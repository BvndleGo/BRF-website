Bvndle Event Terms

Last updated 3 November 2026. These terms apply to the digital card (for example a Bvndle passport or Hero Card), activities and Bvndle coins at any event run with Bvndle.

In short: one card per person, use your real details, and collect your coins at the Bvndle reward desk. For 14 days after the event, Bvndle may cancel or take back coins you earned at the event that you have not yet redeemed.

1. Who these terms are between

These terms are between you and Bvndle. By ticking the box when you get your digital card, you agree to them. If you don't agree, don't get a card or take part in the activities.

2. Your digital card

  1. Each person may hold one card per event. Use your own name, email address and phone number.
  2. Your card is linked to the phone and browser you used to get it. Getting it again with the same email or phone number brings back the same card.
  3. You are responsible for keeping your card to yourself. Don't share your card link or let others use it.

3. Activities and coins

  1. Activities (such as quiz questions, word puzzles and staff codes) are open only while the event is live. Each activity can be completed once per card, and quiz questions allow one attempt.
  2. The coins shown on your card are the coins you have earned at the event. They are collected at the Bvndle reward desk at the event and paid to your Bvndle account.
  3. You need a Bvndle tag (your Bvndle username) to be paid. If you don't have one, you can create one in the Bvndle app.
  4. Coins have no cash value outside the Bvndle app and are subject to the Bvndle app's own terms once paid to your account.

4. Coin review and clawback

  1. Coins earned at an event are provisional for 14 days after the date of that event (the "review period").
  2. During the review period, Bvndle may, at its discretion, cancel, reduce or reclaim any coins earned at the event that have not yet been redeemed. This includes, but is not limited to, where Bvndle reasonably believes coins were earned through:
    1. more than one card per person;
    2. false details, or another person's details;
    3. sharing answers, codes or QR links outside the venue;
    4. technical errors, misuse, or any other breach of these terms.
  3. Coins you have already redeemed will not be reclaimed under this section.
  4. After the review period ends, coins earned at the event are final.
  5. Questions about a review can be sent to help@bvndle.com within the review period. Bvndle's decision on a review is final.

5. Your information

We use your name, email address, phone number and Bvndle tag to issue your card, run the activities, pay your coins and contact you about the event. We don't sell your information.

6. Changes and ending activities

Bvndle may change, pause or end an activity or event at any time, and may update these terms. The version shown when you got your card applies to that event.

7. Contact

Email help@bvndle.com.
