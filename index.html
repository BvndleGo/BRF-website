                Frequently asked questions

                Is it really free?

                Yes. The festival ticket is free and covers the market, games, raffles and brand activities. The Bvndle Rewards Concert is a separate paid ticket on TIX.

                What is Bvndle Rewards Festival?

                A two-day festival where brands reward their loyal customers with games, raffles, prizes, music and experiences.

                When and where is it happening?

                11th – 12th December 2026 at Landmark Event Centre, Lagos.

                What are the rewards, and how do I redeem them?

                Prizes include appliances, gadgets, skincare, merch and cash. Redemption happens instantly at the redemption booths.

                Can I combine festival rewards with other discounts?

                Festival rewards stand alone but may unlock special brand offers.

                Do my points expire after the event?

                Festival-only rewards must be claimed during the event. Your BvndleCoins in the app stay valid.

                What's the ROI of sponsoring a rewards festival?

                Direct engagement with thousands of consumers, measurable brand exposure and loyalty activation.

                Can we co-create custom experiences or offers?

                Yes. Sponsors can design tailored booths, challenges or exclusive giveaways.

                How are rewards distributed and who bears the cost?

                Sponsors provide their rewards. Bvndle handles distribution logistics during the festival.
